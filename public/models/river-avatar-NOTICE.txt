Author: pixiv / VRoid.

The original VRM metadata and embedded textures are retained. Model terms are

Changes in this app: red hair/clothing/shoes for Alexx, purple clothing and a
moderate body/clothing silhouette adjustment for BV. These are applied by the
shared runtime loader, not baked into the unmodified source VRM binaries.
